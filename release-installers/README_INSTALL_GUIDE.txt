3. INSTALLATION OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OPTION A: PORTABLE STANDALONE EXECUTABLE (No Installation Needed)
  1. Open the folder containing "NexDesign.exe".
  2. Double-click "NexDesign.exe" to launch immediately.
  3. You can place or pin "NexDesign.exe" to your Taskbar or Desktop.

OPTION B: WINDOWS SETUP INSTALLER (Recommended)
  1. Double-click "NexDesign_0.11.8_x64-setup.exe".
  2. Follow the on-screen Setup Wizard prompts.
  3. Choose your desired install location (default: Program Files).
  4. Launch NexDesign directly from the Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.

OPTION C: WINDOWS ENTERPRISE MSI PACKAGE
  1. Double-click "NexDesign_0.11.8_x64_en-US.msi".
  2. Ideal for enterprise deployments, silent rollouts, and IT administration.

4. SIGN IN & ACCOUNT ACTIVATION GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: LAUNCH THE APPLICATION
  • Launch NexDesign from your desktop or start menu.
  • If starting for the first time, you will enter the Studio Workspace.

STEP 2: SIGN IN TO YOUR CLOUD ACCOUNT
  • Click the Profile Pill in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, or 
    open the Settings gear (⚙️) on the bottom dock.
  • Select "Sign In" or "Link Account".
  • Enter your registered credentials:
      - Email: Your registered email address (e.g. user@example.com)
      - Password: Your secure account password
  • Click "Sign In".

STEP 3: CLOUD SYNC & OFFLINE LEASE VERIFICATION
  • Once signed in, NexDesign connects to "https://nex-design.online/api", 
    validates your account status, package tier (Starter, Pro, Teams), and 
    issues a cryptographically verified offline lease (P-256 / ES256).
  • Your authentic Waitlist Queue rank (e.g. #14) and profile information 
    will appear in the bottom-left user menu.
  • You can now work seamlessly both ONLINE and OFFLINE.

5. USER PROFILE, SETTINGS & LOGOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• USER & PROFILE SETTINGS:
  Click the Gear icon (⚙️) on the left dock to open the 3-tab settings modal:
  1. Identity: Display name, email, avatar, device ID.
  2. Academic & Career: University, faculty, graduation year, portfolio link.
  3. Environment & OS: Operating system preference, primary design use cases.

• SWITCHING ACCOUNTS:
  Click the bottom profile pill -> "Add Another Account" to link multiple profiles.

• SIGNING OUT:
  Click the bottom profile pill -> Click the red "Sign Out" button at the bottom.
  (You can also click "Sign Out" from inside the Profile Settings modal).
