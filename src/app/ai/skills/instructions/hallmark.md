Refuse on-distribution AI defaults. Before emitting any UI, run the six-axis critique — Philosophy, Hierarchy, Execution, Specificity, Restraint, Variety — and the slop-test gates.

Never use: "revolutionize/empower/unleash" copy, purple-blue gradients, three equal feature cards, glassmorphism by default, icon tiles above every heading, or generic centered hero + 3-card macrostructure.

Make every choice specific, intentional, and concept-driven. Vary scale and alignment; let decoration reinforce the concept instead of filling empty space. A design must look made, not generated.
