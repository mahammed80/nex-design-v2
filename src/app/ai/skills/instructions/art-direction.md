Every new screen starts from a named direction derived from the product, audience, and tone in the brief. Pick the closest direction below (or a deliberate blend of two) and adapt it — do not invent an unrelated mix of fonts and colors.

**Directions (type pairing · palette recipe · shape)**

- Editorial — Fraunces or Playfair Display headings + Inter / Source Sans 3 body · warm paper bg #F6F3EE, ink #1C1A17, one deep accent (oxblood, forest, ink-blue) · sharp 0–4 radius, hairline 1px rules, generous margins, asymmetric columns.
- Precision tool (dev tools, analytics, fintech) — Inter Tight or IBM Plex Sans + JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono for numbers · cool neutral ramp (#0B0D10 → #F7F8FA), one saturated signal accent (electric blue, lime, amber) · 6–8 radius, compact 4/8/12 spacing, tabular figures, 1px borders over shadows.
- Soft consumer (wellness, lifestyle, social) — Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, or DM Sans · tinted pastel surfaces derived from one hue, deep tinted ink (not black) · 16–24 radius, large touch targets, layered soft shadows (0 8 24 accent@12%).
- Luxe / premium — Cormorant Garamond or Bodoni Moda display + Inter / Jost body · near-black #0E0E0E or bone #EFEBE4, metallic or muted accent (#B08D57, #6B705C) · 0 radius, wide letterSpacing on overlines, sparse layout, large imagery.
- Bold / brutalist (creative, events, youth brands) — Space Grotesk, Archivo Black, or Syne · high-contrast pair (black + one loud color), flat fills · 0 radius, thick 2–3px strokes, oversized type that crops at frame edges, grid made visible.
- Calm enterprise (B2B SaaS, admin, healthcare) — Inter or Geist + same family · neutral ramp tinted slightly blue or green, one trustworthy accent · 8–10 radius, consistent 8pt rhythm, strong table and form craft.

**Palette rules**

- Build a 5-step neutral ramp tinted toward the palette temperature (warm or cool): bg, surface, border, secondary ink, primary ink. Never default to #111827 / #6B7280 / #F9FAFB.
- One accent. Use it for the primary action, the focal data point, and active states only — about 5–10% of the surface. A second color only for semantic states (success, warning, danger).
- Dark mode is a direction choice, not a default. Dark surfaces step up in lightness (#0F1012 → #17181B → #202226), never pure black cards on pure black.

**Composition moves that separate designed from generated**

- Size contrast: one element is dramatically larger than everything else (display type, hero number, hero image).
- Alignment with intent: a strong left edge or a clear grid (12-col desktop, 4-col mobile); break it once, on purpose, for the focal element.
- Specific content: real product names, plausible numbers with units, realistic dates and people. Copy sets the tone as much as color does.
- Detail craft: consistent icon stroke weight, numerals aligned in tables, labels on every input, focus/hover/selected states shown where relevant (e.g. one active nav item, one selected row).
- Restraint: if a decoration does not support the concept, remove it.
