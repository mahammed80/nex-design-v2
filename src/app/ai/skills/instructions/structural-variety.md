Derive the macrostructure from the content and the job of the screen, not from a template. Before building, name the layout pattern for each section.

**Never by default:** centered hero followed by three equal feature cards; purple-blue gradients; glassmorphism; icon tiles above every heading; cards nested inside cards; a stat row of four identical tiles with invented metrics; "revolutionize / empower / unleash / seamless" copy; the same section rhythm repeated down the page; duplicated CTAs with the same intent.

**Patterns by screen type (choose, then adapt)**

- Landing page: asymmetric split hero (7/5) with product visual · or oversized editorial headline with one supporting image · or product-UI-first hero. Follow with sections that alternate density and alignment (full-bleed proof → narrow text column → comparison table → single focused CTA).
- Dashboard: left nav or top bar + one primary chart/number that is clearly the largest element + secondary metrics at a smaller scale + a real data table or activity list. Not a grid of equal cards.
- Mobile app screen (390×844): status-bar-safe header, one primary content block, thumb-zone primary action, tab bar only if the app has 3–5 top-level destinations. Content edge-to-edge or 16–20 side padding — pick one.
- Settings / forms: grouped sections with titles and helper text, labels above inputs, one primary action per group, destructive actions separated.
- Auth / onboarding: brand moment on one side or top, a short focused form, one secondary path. No feature checklists.
- Detail / content page: strong title block, readable measure (60–75 characters per line), supporting metadata in a quieter sidebar or row.

**Variety within one design:** vary section scale and alignment deliberately, but keep spacing tokens, radius language, and component behavior identical. Two consecutive designs in the same document should differ in macrostructure and hero archetype.
